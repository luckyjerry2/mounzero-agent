Decision Engine

Two outcomes, no vague advice.

BLOCKED

Emotion plus unnecessary spend

Stress, boredom, craving, delivery food, sugary drinks, junk food, and impulse buying trigger a high-risk stop.

Output: alternative action + saved amount.

APPROVED

Clear need or essential purchase

School, work, health, bills, transportation, or essential living can proceed after wallet and payment confirmation.

Output: x402 payment-required step.

Implementation

A demo stack that can become onchain.
